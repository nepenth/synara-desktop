Synara 2.5.0 brings GIFs and X posts to iOS. GIF links play in the conversation and GIF attachments animate. Tap Attach → GIF, or type /gif or /gif high five, to search Klipy and send a GIF. x.com links show the post with its photos and tap-to-play video. GIF search, inline GIFs and X posts are on by default; turn them off in Settings → GIFs & Link Previews. When the homeserver is unreachable, "Connection Lost! Reconnecting..." now shows until it answers again.

Please test: searching and sending a GIF (including in a reply and a thread), GIFs and X posts sent from desktop, X post video, scrolling a room with many GIFs, and the Connection Lost banner while the server or network is down.
