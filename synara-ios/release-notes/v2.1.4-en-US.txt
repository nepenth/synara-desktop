Synara 2.1.4 makes failed and offline sends recoverable, adds long-press copy and edit for unsent messages, and holds Connection Lost through short blips.

What to test:
- Upgrade from 2.1.3 and confirm your session restores without another login.
- Send a text while offline: the row should queue, then send when you are back. Failed sends show a Retry chip; tapping it must resend. Long-press the row for Copy.
- Edit a failed unsent message, save, and confirm it retries on the same bubble instead of creating a duplicate.
- Toggle airplane mode for 1–2 seconds: Connection Lost must not flash. Hold Offline for about 4 seconds and confirm Lost appears.
- On desktop: unread counts and right-click Mark as Read should clear. Typing in a room should push the last message up. Message rows should have a full-width slightly grey background.
- Sign out, relaunch, and sign in again.

Please report the room type, action, and approximate time for any send, copy, unread, or connection issue. Do not include access tokens or recovery keys.
