Synara 3.5.1 changes when consecutive messages from one person are shown as a group. A message sent 5 minutes or more after that person's previous message now shows their name, picture and time again. Before, messages stayed under one name and time for up to two hours.

Please test: open a room where one person sent several messages in a row over a longer time. Check that messages sent within 5 minutes of each other share one name and time, and that a message sent after a longer pause starts with the name and time again.
