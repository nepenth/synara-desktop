Synara 2.1.19 makes the iOS interface's quiet dimensionality clearly visible while keeping it restrained and readable.

What changed:
- Room rows and message bodies now use real layered surfaces instead of appearing flat.
- Avatars, reactions, message actions, and the composer have a clearer visual hierarchy.
- Agent, encrypted, deleted, and unsupported messages receive stronger emphasis than ordinary conversation.
- Message actions now have a visible, accessible 44-point button, while long press remains available.
- Vertical room-history scrolling and grouped-message timestamp reveal remain independent and responsive.
- Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency use accessible boundary and shadow behavior.

What to test:
- Compare Rooms and a room timeline in both light and dark mode.
- Scroll a busy room vertically, then swipe a grouped message left to reveal its timestamp.
- Open the visible message actions button and confirm Copy, Reply, React, and Pin to Notes as available.
- Type a paragraph while new messages arrive and confirm input remains responsive.
