Synara 2.1.10 improves readability and responsiveness across the app.

What changed:
- Room lists use compact channel markers and clearer # Room Name labels.
- Softer dark-mode colors, spacing, tables, replies, and message typography make long conversations easier to read.
- Drag to reorder synced room notes and keep their ordering across clients.
- Message composition does less synchronous layout work for more responsive typing.
- Dynamic Type, notification disclosure, surface continuity, and floating-tab-bar clearance are improved.

What to test:
- Reorder room notes on iOS and confirm their order on another signed-in client.
- Type rapidly or compose a long message at standard and very large text sizes.
- Review rooms, Settings, and technical messages in both light and dark appearance.
