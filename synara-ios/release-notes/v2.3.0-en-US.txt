Synara 2.3.0 fixes more than 200 issues found in a full review. Messages are never lost or sent twice, rooms stay live while you read history, and notifications open the right room or thread with its history. Invites now appear in Rooms and Notifications. The long-press menu has quick reactions, and tapping a reaction adds or removes yours. Cross-signing is set up when you sign in, Settings → Security can set up recovery, and Reset Identity helps an account that lost its recovery key. Approve once now asks you to unlock unless you turn on "Approve once without unlocking". Read receipts are now public unless "Hide Typing & Read Receipts" is on.

Please test: sending while offline or during a weak connection, invites, quick reactions, VoiceOver in a room, and opening rooms from notifications.
