Synara 2.1.15 fixes the remaining iOS suspension crash and aligns the macOS composer with your message-text setting.

- Foreground/background transitions are serialized and duplicate suspension callbacks safely coalesce.

- Added lifecycle regressions and reusable TestFlight crash diagnostics.
- The macOS message composer now follows the selected Soft, Balanced, or Bright message-text tone.
