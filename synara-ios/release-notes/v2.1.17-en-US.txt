Synara 2.1.17 makes busy room timelines calmer and restores sender identity without requiring a new login.

What changed:
- Consecutive messages from the same sender now form readable two-hour groups on iOS, macOS, and Linux.
- Repeated timestamps stay hidden and can be revealed with a leftward gesture.
- Sender profiles hydrate in the background so avatars and display names update during the active session.
- The desktop message composer now uses one clean circular add button without the dark square backing.

What to test:
- Open a busy room and confirm consecutive messages are visually grouped.
- Swipe left on a grouped message to reveal its timestamp.
- Confirm avatars populate without signing out and back in.
- On macOS, confirm the add button has no dark square behind it.
