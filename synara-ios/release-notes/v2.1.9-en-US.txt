Synara 2.1.9 adds synced room notes and opt-in encrypted notification previews on iOS.

What changed:
- Open Notes from a room timeline and keep room notes synced through your Matrix account.
- When message previews are enabled, notifications can include content from encrypted rooms.
- Notification delivery is cancellation-safe and falls back to generic text when content cannot be resolved in time.
- Device verification retains its Verified trust result after session restore and relaunch.
- Settings content remains clear of the floating tab bar.

What to test:
- Edit a room note on iOS and confirm it appears on another signed-in client.
- Compare notifications with message previews enabled and disabled in clear and encrypted rooms.
- Relaunch both clients after device verification and confirm the exact peer still reads Verified.

Never approve device verification when the comparison differs.
