Synara 2.1.13 fixes a crash that could occur while viewing an actively updating room and improves typing responsiveness in busy conversations.

What changed:
- Timeline updates are serialized and coalesced to prevent a UIKit snapshot crash.
- Formatted messages no longer invoke a run-loop-pumping decoder while rendering.
- Redundant and unchanged timeline refreshes are removed before reaching the screen.
- Long message drafts avoid repeated full-text layout work on every keystroke.
- Privacy-safe performance diagnostics help identify any remaining stalls without recording message content.

What to test:
- Read a busy room while new formatted messages arrive.
- Type and edit a long paragraph while the room is updating.
- Submit any TestFlight crash prompt if one appears.
