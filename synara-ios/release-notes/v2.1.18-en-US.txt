Synara 2.1.18 adds subtle visual depth across the app and restores dependable room-history scrolling.

What changed:
- Room rows, composers, message controls, avatars, and message surfaces now use restrained depth to make interactive areas easier to distinguish.
- Vertical scrolling in busy room histories remains responsive, including when a drag begins on a grouped message.
- A deliberate leftward swipe on a grouped message still reveals its timestamp without competing with ordinary scrolling.
- Live timeline updates can no longer leave a stale timestamp gesture or visible message cell behind.

What to test:
- Scroll quickly up and down through a busy room, starting drags directly on grouped messages.
- Swipe a grouped message left and confirm its timestamp appears, then returns cleanly.
- Review Rooms, a room timeline, the composer, message actions, and approval prompts in light and dark mode.
