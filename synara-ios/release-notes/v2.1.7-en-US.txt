Synara 2.1.7 fixes device verification and improves readability in Dark Mode.

What changed:
- Device verification now reaches the emoji or decimal comparison, completes after both people confirm, and refreshes the trusted session state.
- Comparison starts only when you choose Start Comparison, and the verification sheet stays readable while secure state changes arrive.
- The last rows in Settings remain visible and tappable above the floating tab bar.
- Messages use more comfortable body text. Wide tables scroll horizontally and use clearer headers, separators, and alternating rows.

What to test:
- From Account → Sessions, verify another signed-in device and confirm that both devices show the same comparison before approving.
- In Dark Mode, scroll to the bottom of each Settings screen and confirm every row remains above the tab bar.
- Open a long technical message with headings, lists, code, and a table.

Never approve verification when the comparison differs. Please report the client, action, and approximate time for any issue; do not include passwords, access tokens, or recovery keys.
