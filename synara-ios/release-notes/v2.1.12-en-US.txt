Synara 2.1.12 fixes iOS crashes that could occur after the app moved to the background.

What changed:
- Synara now waits for its native Matrix sync service to stop before iOS suspends the app.
- Returning to the foreground safely restarts the same signed-in session after shutdown completes.
- Rapid app switching can no longer trigger a stale sync restart while Synara is backgrounded.
- New privacy-safe lifecycle diagnostics help identify any remaining suspension issue without recording message or account data.

What to test:
- Background and foreground Synara repeatedly while rooms are actively receiving messages.
- Switch rapidly between Synara and other apps, then confirm rooms and timelines resume normally.
- Submit any TestFlight crash prompt if one appears.
