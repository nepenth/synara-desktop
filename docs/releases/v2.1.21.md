# Synara 2.1.21

Synara 2.1.21 hardens timeline restoration and makes cross-client attachment
sending faithful to Matrix protocol semantics.

## Offline Timeline Restoration

- Opening a persisted native timeline no longer requires a homeserver round
  trip to determine encryption state.
- A production-shaped test now syncs an event into the encrypted SQLite SDK
  stores, destroys the client, takes the homeserver offline, restores a fresh
  client from the same stores, and proves the cached event is readable.
- Initial pagination is bounded and reports an authoritative empty room only
  after the SDK cache is exhausted.
- iOS preserves the last readable rows during a timeline failure and exposes a
  dedicated, privacy-safe retry banner instead of presenting the failure as a
  composer send error.
- Stale native timeline views recover once per room, avoid replacing newer
  streams, and emit a recovery heartbeat even when the restored rows are
  unchanged.

## Attachment Semantics and Retry Safety

- Filenames and captions remain distinct across Rust, UniFFI, Swift, Tauri,
  and TypeScript.
- Captions carry formatted content, Matrix mentions, explicit thread roots,
  reply policy, and caller-owned transaction IDs through the native send path.
- The native and client upload boundaries consistently accept at most 32 MiB.
- Multi-attachment sends assign composer text exactly once. Partial retries
  preserve whether that text is a caption or a trailing message, allow edits,
  suppress cleared text, and safely assign text introduced after a partial
  attachment-only send.
- Successfully uploaded drafts are removed individually, so retrying never
  re-sends completed attachments.

## Stickers and Image Packs

- Unsupported outgoing sticker-send controls and callable native APIs were
  removed from iOS, desktop, Tauri, UniFFI, and shared core.
- Incoming `m.sticker` events continue to project and render as media.
- Custom emoji, reactions, and existing mixed or sticker image-pack metadata
  remain intact when packs are edited.

## Room List Accessibility

- iOS room rows prioritize the room title and move compact status information
  out of its layout path.
- VoiceOver receives a combined title and status description.
- Accessibility XL UI coverage verifies that titles remain visible and inside
  the row bounds.

## Validation

- Desktop TypeScript type checking, ESLint, Prettier, and 846 modernization
  tests passed.
- Focused iOS timeline, composer, media, and generated-binding suites passed;
  the final composer retry suite passed 23 tests.
- The production-shaped offline cold-restart proof, Rust media integration,
  incoming sticker behavior, Cargo checks, Tauri checks, Swift scaffold
  contract, Rust formatting, and diff checks passed.
- Three independent delegated review lanes accepted the timeline, media, and
  sticker/UI work. Grok 4.6 High reported no merge blockers, identified one
  adjacent retry edge case, and accepted the tested correction.

## What to Test

- Open a previously synced room in airplane mode after terminating the app and
  confirm cached messages appear without a server connection.
- Retry after a partial multi-file upload while editing, clearing, or newly
  typing the retained text; confirm files are not duplicated and text is sent
  exactly once.
- Send a captioned file in a reply and in a thread, then verify its filename,
  caption, relation, and mentions in another Matrix client.
- Review incoming stickers and edit existing mixed image packs to confirm their
  metadata and reaction use remain intact.
- Increase iOS text size to Accessibility XL and confirm room titles and status
  remain readable.
