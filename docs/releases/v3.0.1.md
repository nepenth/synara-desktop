# Synara 3.0.1

Synara 3.0.1 keeps the message composer in view in short tiles on Linux tiling window managers, tightens the space under the composer, and refines the new Synara mark.

## Desktop

- Composer: the empty strip under the message composer is now a 12px gap instead of 28px.
- Synara mark: the centre ring is joined to the rest of the mark, with two spokes to the nodes on the arc and the ring set against the wing. Updated in the app, the app icons, the tray icon and the Windows installer.

## Linux

- Tiling window managers: in a tile shorter than 480px the composer was cut off below the window. Synara no longer asks for a 480px minimum height there (the floor is 240×160), and the composer stays in view.

## iOS and iPad

- Updated app icon and in-app mark.

## Known limits

- The short-tile fix was checked with a floating window resized down to 160px under Hyprland, not in an actual tile of that height.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers (checked to launch to the sign-in screen first), and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
