# Synara 2.8.1

Synara 2.8.1 makes the room list easier to scan on every platform, simplifies the desktop rail, and fixes two long-standing desktop annoyances: text typed after a pasted link no longer becomes part of the link, and a macOS in-app update no longer leaves a second Synara icon in the Dock.

## Desktop

- Room list: read rooms recede to a quieter grey, unread rooms are bold with a short bar at the left edge, muted rooms are dimmer still, and the selected room sits on a clearly filled row. Section headers are small captions with a visible chevron and more air above each group. Long room names keep their ending ("Project - Hom…agement"). Direct messages show a presence dot: green when the person is online, grey when offline.
- Rail: Home, spaces, Activity, Approvals and your account. The Direct messages, Explore, "+" and Search buttons are gone; create, join, new message, explore and create space live in the "+" menu at the top of the list, and the search field opens the global switcher (⌘K).
- Composer: typing after a pasted link, with or without a space first, writes plain text instead of extending the link.
- Updates (macOS): after an in-app update the app quits and reopens through the system once the old process has gone, so the Dock keeps your pinned icon instead of adding a second one.

## iOS and iPad

- Rooms: the same reading hierarchy as desktop, with the unread bar, caption headers with a chevron, middle-truncated names, and presence dots on direct messages. On iPad the selected room fills with the accent colour.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers (checked to launch to the sign-in screen first), and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
