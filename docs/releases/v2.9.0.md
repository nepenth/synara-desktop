# Synara 2.9.0

Synara 2.9.0 lets an unsent message follow you between your devices, rebuilds message formatting so lists, quotes and code behave predictably, explains and speeds up slow history loading, and adds a connection beam that shows when Synara is reconnecting.

## Desktop

- Drafts: start typing in a room on one device and finish on another. Drafts are end-to-end encrypted in your room account data, and a newer draft from another device never overwrites what you are typing: Synara asks first. Turn it off per device in Settings → General.
- Formatting: Shift+Enter continues a numbered or bulleted list, numbering carries on after a note in between, Backspace removes a line's formatting before joining lines, and toolbar formats apply to every selected line. An empty list no longer leaves a stray dot beside the message placeholder.
- History: Jump to Last Read shows a spinner while it opens and says why when it cannot, instead of "not available in this context". Loading older messages keeps going until there is something to read, even in rooms full of joins or bridge activity. A slow first load says so and offers Try again.
- Connection: a thin beam under the titlebar pulses red while Synara reconnects, turns green when it is back, then fades. It replaces the reconnecting and connected text banners.

## iOS and iPad

- Drafts: the room composer syncs with your other devices, with formatting carried as Markdown. Turn it off in Settings → Security & Recovery.
- Formatting: Return continues lists and quotes, numbering continues from the list above, and switching formats replaces the marker instead of stacking it.
- Connection: the same beam runs under the room list, room and thread headers. "Connection Lost!" with Retry now sits below those headers instead of underneath them.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers (checked to launch to the sign-in screen first), and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
