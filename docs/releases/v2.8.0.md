# Synara 2.8.0

Synara 2.8.0 aligns the desktop, iPhone and iPad apps around one Slack-informed conversation and navigation model: the same message actions in the same order, one room list that holds rooms and direct messages, red badges only where something needs you, and the same words, colours and type on every platform.

## Desktop

- Room list: rooms and direct messages are one list. The "Direct messages" section sits under Rooms, sorted by activity and collapsible; the Direct messages rail button jumps to that section instead of swapping the whole list. Rows are 15 px like the timeline; an unread room is bold, a mention or an unread direct message gets a red count badge, and a plain unread shows a dot. Muted rooms are dimmed with a slashed bell, and private, invite-only or restricted rooms show a lock. A search field replaces the three utility rows, with create and join behind a "+" menu. Space lists gain sort, favourites and empty states. The app opens on the room you were last in.
- Messages: hovering a message shows React, Reply, Reply in thread, Edit (your own), Forward, Save for later and More, in that order, with arrow-key focus. Times appear in the avatar column on hover. Edited messages say "(edited)" inline. "New messages" sits on a red rule. Replies quote the original with a thin bar, in the timeline and in the composer preview. System events are quiet italic rows, and every room starts with a short intro. The send button is filled when there is something to send, and "jump to latest" shows how many new messages wait.
- Settings and dialogs: log out, leave, remove avatar, room upgrade, diagnostics and updater prompts share one confirmation dialog. Errors show inline under the field, and every field has a visible label. Appearance has a segmented theme row; Devices is split into "Security & recovery" and "Sessions"; Notifications uses switches that save as you change them; Approvals has one heading and a consistent Deny/Approve order. Everything is in sentence case.
- Activity: the rail button and pane formerly called "Inbox" are now "Activity", as on iOS.
- People: a person's fallback avatar colour is the same here as on iOS, and avatars follow one size ladder in member lists, the reaction viewer and your profile.

## iOS and iPad

- Rooms: one 44 pt header with a title menu for spaces and sort replaces the stacked chip rows, so a 6.1" phone shows eleven rooms at once. Sections collapse and remember their state, spaces show their unread counts in the title menu, muted rooms are dimmed, and the lock means encrypted. The first section is "Rooms", not "Channels". Red badges appear only for mentions and direct messages.
- Activity: the "Notifications" tab is now "Activity" (Settings > Notifications keeps its name). Approvals is a labelled row at the top of it. Links to the old name still open it.
- Messages: swipe a message to reply. Reactions wrap as pills at the same size as desktop; your own is tinted in the accent colour. The long-press menu lists React, Reply, Reply in thread, Edit, Forward, Save to Later and More, in the desktop order, with "Delete" last. Reply quotes are tappable thin-bar quotes; thread chips show who replied and when; edited messages say "(edited)" inline; mentions of you are gold. The composer uses body type and an animated send button.
- Later: you can now save a message to Later from the long-press menu, into the same list desktop uses. Later rows show an excerpt.
- iPad: Settings is a sidebar and a detail pane, and the room sidebar collapses. The connection banner shows only when you can act on it.
- The accent colour matches desktop's blue in light and dark.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers (checked to launch to the sign-in screen first), and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
