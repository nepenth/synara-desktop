# Synara 3.3.0

Synara 3.3.0 adds folders for rooms on desktop and iOS, makes the room list's bold mean unread only, and makes window shortcuts work from the first frame.

## Room folders (desktop and iOS)

- Group rooms into your own folders, such as "Work" or "Side projects". Folders are sections in the room list after Favorites, in your order, and collapse like the other sections; a collapsed folder shows its unread or mention count.
- File a room from its menu: Move to folder, with New folder… and Remove from folder. On desktop, Home options and a space's options also have New folder; on iOS it is in the list's title menu.
- Rename, move up or down, and delete a folder from its header menu. Deleting a folder returns its rooms to Rooms.
- Folders are private to you and sync across your devices through your Matrix account data. Devices running an older Synara ignore them.
- Rules: a room is in one folder at most; direct messages and spaces cannot be filed; Favorites stays a built-in section and wins (a favorite room shows under Favorites and returns to its folder when unfavorited); up to 100 folders, names up to 64 characters.

## Desktop

- The selected row in the room list is no longer bold. Bold means only that a room has something unread; the room you are in is marked by its fill.
- Room, space, room-header and lobby menus use sentence case ("Mark as read", "Copy link", "Room settings").
- Keyboard shortcuts work from the moment the window shows. A shortcut pressed as the room list first appeared could do nothing.
- Every section of the Home room list is now a virtualised list, so a folder or Favorites with hundreds of rooms stays fast.
- Dependency updates: Slate 0.147 (the composer's editor), i18next 27 and react-i18next 18, pdf.js 6.5, Tauri plugins (updater 2.14.1, notification 2.8.0) and build tooling.

## iOS

- Reading a cached room while offline no longer makes the timeline jump as connection banners appear and disappear.
- On iPad, a collapsed room-list section keeps the open conversation visible, as desktop does. Section headers are headings for VoiceOver.

## Known limits

- Folders were tested on harnesses, mock data and a mock homeserver, not against a live homeserver or between two real devices, and not in the packaged apps.
- If two devices change folders at the same moment, the last change wins for the whole folder list. A change made on another device within about 30 seconds of a local change can take up to a minute to show.
- Folders are filed and reordered through menus; there is no drag and drop yet.
- Emoji data (emojibase) and the list virtualiser (`@tanstack/react-virtual`) were held at their current versions; the newer ones need follow-up work first.
- The iOS app is still built with the iOS 26 SDK; the move to the iOS 27 SDK is in progress and is not in this release.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers, and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
