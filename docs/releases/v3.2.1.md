# Synara 3.2.1

Synara 3.2.1 groups iOS Settings the way desktop does and keeps a composer error from taking over the desktop window, and fixes silent videos on Linux.

## iOS

- Settings are grouped like desktop's: your account at the top, then App (General, Appearance, Notifications), Chat (Messages, Media & links, Privacy), Account (Security & recovery, Sessions) and Advanced (Advanced, Diagnostics, About), with Log out last. No setting was removed and every setting keeps its stored value.
- Rows moved to where desktop has them: 24-hour time is on General; Sync drafts across devices is on Messages; GIF search, Play GIF links inline, Link previews, Show X posts and Shorten long links are on Media & links; Hide typing & read receipts and Blocked users are on Privacy; the session list is on Sessions; the call service status is on Advanced; the push delivery log is on Diagnostics, still linked from Notifications; Licenses, Privacy policy and Support are rows of About.
- Section and row names use sentence case and match desktop's.

## Desktop

- If the message box runs into an error, it no longer replaces the whole screen with "Something went wrong". It reloads in place with your draft, reply and attachments. If it fails again right away, a one-line notice takes its place with Try again, Start fresh and Copy draft. The same applies to the thread pane composer and to editing a message in place.

## Linux

- MP4 videos (video attachments and the video in an X post) could play with no sound when the system had no AAC decoder. The pacman package now depends on `gst-libav`, and the .deb on `gstreamer1.0-libav` and `gstreamer1.0-plugins-good`.

## Known limits

- The Linux audio fix was diagnosed on one Arch machine (no AAC decoder installed) but not confirmed by installing the package there.

- The iOS layout was tested on an iPhone simulator. The iPad split view was not checked, and nothing was tried on a device.
- The composer safety net was tested in browser harnesses, not in the packaged app. Only errors raised while the composer renders are contained; those were the only ones that reached the full-screen page.
- After Start fresh, other devices can keep the old synced draft until the next edit.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers, and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
