# Synara 2.3.0

Synara 2.3.0 is a reliability release built from a full review of both apps, the Rust core and its use of the Matrix SDK. Messages are no longer lost or sent twice, rooms stay live while you read history, notifications and links open the right room or thread, invites appear on iOS, and both apps gain account recovery, accessibility and security fixes.

## Messages and rooms

- Every message goes through a persistent send queue. A message sent while Synara is connecting, offline or about to be closed goes out once the connection is back, exactly once. A message the server refuses for good offers only Discard; one that can still succeed offers Retry.
- Notifications and links to a message open its room with the history around it and keep receiving new messages. A reply in a thread opens that thread at the reply, with the room behind it.
- Removing a message now asks "Remove this message? This can't be undone." first, on desktop and iOS.
- Read receipts are now public, so people see how far you have read, unless "Hide Typing & Read Receipts" is on.
- Typing stays visible to others for as long as you type, and an edit that adds a mention notifies that person.
- Signing in or starting with an empty cache no longer fetches history for every room, and mention badges stay accurate after signing in or resuming.

## iOS

- Room and direct-message invites appear in Rooms and Notifications, where you can accept or decline them.
- Rooms stay live while you read earlier messages, coming back to a room keeps your place, and messages from different days get day headers.
- The long-press menu has a row of quick reactions, shared with desktop's recent emoji, and tapping a reaction adds or removes yours.
- Sign-in finds your homeserver through `.well-known`, says why a sign-in failed, and refuses servers without simplified sliding sync (MSC4186) with an explanation.
- Notification previews and Approve/Deny keep working after Synara has been in the background for a while.
- Control Center, Notification Center and system alerts no longer disconnect Synara, and it reconnects at once after a network change.
- Agent rooms are dark only inside the conversation.
- VoiceOver reads the timeline in order, reads each message as one item with its reactions, and offers message actions in the rotor. Larger text sizes wrap instead of truncating.

## Desktop

- Click an image to view it full size, or open a video in the viewer, and save the original. Photos and videos from iPhones (HEIC and QuickTime) load, and audio and video can be seeked.
- Direct messages show the other person's avatar or initial instead of `#`.
- The composer never loses or reorders messages, and a failed send puts its text back in the box with the reason.
- Notifications open the conversation, including direct messages and rooms in spaces, also right after launch or after signing back in. Sign-out clears Synara's notifications and badges. Approval alerts still arrive with notifications off or Do Not Disturb on, at most three a minute per room.
- The global shortcuts default to Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M / L / N (Cmd+Option+Shift on macOS) and can each be turned off. Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ move between rooms, and Cmd/Ctrl + = / − / 0 and Cmd/Ctrl + , work.
- Dialogs, menus, focus and contrast meet WCAG AA, and about 100 icon-only controls now have names.
- Linux: on desktops without a tray, closing the window quits Synara, and downloads go to your localized Downloads folder.
- Timeline updates reach each open view over its own channel, and much less work runs in the background.

## Encryption and security

- Signing in to an account without cross-signing sets it up on that device. On iOS, Settings → Security can set up recovery and shows the recovery key once.
- Reset Identity lets an account that has lost its recovery key and every other device start over, and new devices are asked to verify themselves.
- On iOS, Approve once asks you to unlock first unless you turn on "Approve once without unlocking". Once you list your agents, only they can raise urgent approval alerts.
- Opening a room no longer starts a loop of encryption-status requests.
- The desktop content security policy is tighter, and registration CAPTCHAs open in your browser.
- Dependency security updates: rustls, rustls-webpki and webpki-root-certs (RUSTSEC-2026-0346 to 0349, 0382 and 0383), zip removed from the desktop updater (RUSTSEC-2026-0367 to 0369), and sanitize-html 2.18.1 (GHSA-c9rx-x947-fp3h).

## Changes to know about

- Read receipts are public unless "Hide Typing & Read Receipts" is on.
- The desktop global shortcuts have new defaults; settings that still used the old defaults move to the new ones.
- On iOS, Approve once needs your iPhone unlocked unless you turn on "Approve once without unlocking".
- Servers without simplified sliding sync are refused at sign-in, and single sign-on is not supported yet.
- "Sign Out" and "Logout" now read "Log Out" on both apps.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed/notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
