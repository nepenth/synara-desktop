# Synara 3.2.0

Synara 3.2.0 reorganizes desktop Settings by task, cleans up message timestamps, shows a short "What's new" after an update on every platform, and makes the iPhone Settings rows work again.

## Desktop

- Settings are grouped by what you are doing: App (General, Appearance, Notifications), Chat (Messages, Media & links, Privacy), Account (Account, Security & recovery, Sessions) and Advanced (Advanced, Diagnostics, About). No setting was removed and nothing you had set changes. GIF search, link previews and media loading are together under Media & links; experimental and maintenance items are under Advanced.
- Settings search understands phrases such as "enable gif", "turn on gifs" or "block someone", ranks its results, and Settings reopens on the page you last viewed.
- The profile picture in the bottom bar opens Settings directly and closes it when clicked again, as Cmd/Ctrl+, does. Its menu is gone; Log out is in the Settings sidebar. With an unverified session it opens Security & recovery.
- The bottom bar no longer reloads when you switch between Home, Activity and Approvals, so the profile picture no longer flashes its initial and the verification warning no longer blinks.
- The composer's "+" menu keeps its GIF item when GIF search is off and offers to turn it on.
- Message times: the time beside a sender's name is the time alone, such as "2:03 PM"; the date is in a short day heading ("Today", "Yesterday", "Nov 14th"). Hovering a message no longer shows a date tooltip over the text, and follow-on messages no longer show a time in the left margin. Point at the time beside the name for the full date. Twelve-hour times have no leading zero.
- After an update, a small "What's new" card in the corner of the window lists the release's headline and up to four changes, once. The macOS and Windows update prompt lists the same notes, and Settings → About keeps them.
- The timeline no longer shows a frame at the wrong scroll position when a message grows in WebKit.

## iOS

- The rows on the iPhone Settings screen open their pages again; since 2.8.0 tapping them did nothing. iPad was unaffected.
- After an update, a "What's New" card at the top of the Rooms list shows the release's headline and changes, once; About keeps them.

## Known limits

- None of the desktop changes were tried in the packaged app; they were tested in browser harnesses on Chromium and WebKit. That covers the avatar's click-again-to-close timing, the profile picture and warning badge staying put, and the real update prompt.
- The iOS settings layout does not yet match the new desktop grouping.
- What's new: someone signed out while updating is treated as a fresh install and does not see that release's card; after a crash the card can show once more. On iOS it was tested in the simulator, not on a device or through a TestFlight update.
- The new card covers the top corner of the members or thread pane until it is dismissed.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers, and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
