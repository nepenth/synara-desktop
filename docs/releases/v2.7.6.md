# Synara 2.7.6

Synara 2.7.6 brings the 2.7.5 iOS timeline restyle to desktop, sized for a desktop window, and tidies the iPad room header.

## Desktop

- Each day starts with a bold heading ("Today", "Yesterday", or the weekday and date) over a full-width rule.
- Sender names are bold, and message text is a little smaller and tighter.
- Reactions are roomier pills, yours outlined in the accent colour, followed by an add-reaction pill that opens the emoji picker.
- A mention is a pill in the text, gold when it is you and blue for anyone else. The tinted band across a whole message that mentions you is gone.
- The room title is bold, and the composer reads "Message #room" in a channel or "Message Name" in a DM.

## iPad

- The room title capsule fits the room name instead of stretching across the pane.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers (checked to launch to the sign-in screen first), and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
