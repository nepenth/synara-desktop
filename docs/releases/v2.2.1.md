# Synara 2.2.1

Synara 2.2.1 fixes issues found in 2.2.0 on Linux and iOS, redesigns Settings, and adds a choice of how threads open.

## Fixes

- Linux: the window's title strip with Minimize, Maximize and Close is back, and the window drags from it again on Wayland.
- iOS: returning to the room list after opening a room from a notification shows your rooms instead of placeholder rows.
- Timeline: the normal scrollbar returns. The 7-day date rail, which covered it and placed its dots by time rather than scroll position, is removed.
- Message hover is a steady, calm tint instead of a lifted card.
- Opening the room Call menu while calls are unavailable no longer shows an error screen, and opening a space no longer crashes.
- Room and space menus use one consistent item style.

## Settings

- App, Room and Space settings share one layout: grouped navigation with search, a centered column with one card per section, aligned controls, and a Danger Zone for room upgrades.

## Threads

- Settings → Appearance → Thread Display: open threads in full view (as before), in a resizable side panel next to the room, or inline beneath their first message.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed/notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
