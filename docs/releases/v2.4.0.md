# Synara 2.4.0

Synara 2.4.0 brings GIFs back to the desktop app with Klipy, plays X post videos in the conversation, adds password change, and fixes new rooms showing as "Empty Room".

## Desktop

- GIF search uses Klipy, since Tenor's API shut down. The picker opens on trending GIFs and loads more as you scroll, and `/gif` or `/gif high five` opens it with that search. GIFs are sent as Klipy links, as Klipy's terms require, and Synara plays them inline.
- With "Show X Posts" turned on in Settings → General, x.com links show the post and play its video in place.
- Settings → Account can change your password, and can sign out your other devices at the same time. Servers that manage accounts on their own site, such as matrix.org, open that page instead.
- A new room no longer stays "Empty Room" in the room list.

## Changes to know about

- Inline GIFs and X posts load straight from Klipy, GIPHY and X, which can see your IP address. Turn off "Play GIF Links Inline" or leave "Show X Posts" off to avoid that.
- Other Matrix apps show a GIF sent from Synara as a link.
- The iOS app is unchanged apart from its version number.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed/notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
