# Synara 2.5.0

Synara 2.5.0 brings GIFs and X post previews to the iOS app, turns them on by default on every client, and fixes `/gif` and the GIF search field on desktop.

## iOS

- GIF links from Klipy and GIPHY play in the conversation, and GIF attachments animate. Reduce Motion shows their first frame.
- The Attach sheet has a GIF option, and `/gif` or `/gif high five` opens a Klipy picker with trending GIFs and search. GIFs are sent as Klipy links, as Klipy's terms require.
- x.com and twitter.com post links show the post, with its photos and tap-to-play video.
- Settings → GIFs & Link Previews has "GIF Search", "Play GIF Links Inline" and "Show X Posts".

## Desktop

- Typing `/gif` or `/gif <search>` and pressing Enter opens the GIF picker. Before, the text was sent as a message or only became a command chip.
- The GIF picker's search field fills its row instead of shrinking to a few pixels.

## Changes to know about

- GIF search, inline GIF links and X posts are on by default on desktop and iOS. Desktop turns GIF search and X posts on once, even if an earlier version had them off; turn them off again in Settings → General.
- Inline GIFs and X posts load straight from Klipy, GIPHY and X, which can see your IP address.
- Other Matrix apps show a GIF sent from Synara as a link.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed/notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, and the internal iOS TestFlight build, which now carries the Klipy key.
