# Synara 2.2.2

Synara 2.2.2 makes Settings search find individual settings and opens threads inline by default.

## Settings

- Search finds individual settings across App, Room and Space settings, not just page names. Results show each setting with its description, grouped by page; choosing one opens the page and highlights the setting.

## Threads

- Threads open inline beneath their first message by default. A choice made in Settings → Appearance → Thread Display is kept.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed/notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
