# Synara 2.7.5

Synara 2.7.5 gives the iOS room timeline a more polished conversation view and lets you mention people from the iOS composer, as on desktop.

## iOS

- Avatars are larger rounded squares, sender names are bold with a smaller time beside them, and each day starts with a bold left-aligned heading over a rule.
- Your own messages carry your name instead of "You". VoiceOver still says "You".
- Reactions are roomier pills, followed by an add-reaction pill that offers your recent emoji.
- A mention is a pill in the text, gold when it is you and blue for anyone else, instead of a highlight across the whole message.
- The room header is a back button, a title capsule and a search and details capsule (Liquid Glass on iOS 26 and later). The composer is one rounded field with the attach button inside, reading "Message #room" or "Message Name".
- Typing "@" in the composer lists the room's members who match what follows it; picking one inserts "@Name". The message goes out with the same mention pill desktop sends, and the people it mentions are notified. Editing a message keeps its mentions.

## Desktop

No desktop changes; desktop builds are versioned 2.7.5 with the rest of the release.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers (checked to launch to the sign-in screen first), and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
