# Synara 2.2.6

Synara 2.2.6 makes agent approval notifications actionable: Approve once and Deny now work from the iPhone lock screen, from a paired Apple Watch, and reliably on macOS and Linux.

## iOS and Apple Watch

- Approve once and Deny run without unlocking or opening Synara, so they work from the lock screen and from approval alerts mirrored to Apple Watch. Approve always still requires the app.
- If a decision cannot be sent (for example, the prompt expired), Synara shows an "Approval not sent" notification so you know from your wrist.
- Apple Watch shows iPhone alerts while the iPhone is locked or asleep and the Watch is worn. For approval pushes in encrypted agent rooms, keep the room's notifications on All messages: the homeserver cannot see mentions in encrypted rooms.

## Desktop

- macOS: the first approval after launch no longer arrives without its Approve and Deny buttons. Choose Alerts in System Settings → Notifications → Synara to keep the buttons visible without hovering.
- Approval alerts are no longer removed when the agent room is selected and the window gains focus.
- Linux: when the notification server cannot show buttons, the alert says to open Synara to approve or deny.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed/notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
