# Synara 2.7.4

Synara 2.7.4 makes the iOS room timeline easier to read.

## iOS

- Messages are flat rows instead of outlined pills. The text sits directly under the sender's name, messages from one sender stack closely, and a new sender starts a clear gap.
- The "…" button on every message is gone. Long-press anywhere on a message for its actions, including quick reactions. With VoiceOver or Switch Control on, the button is still there.
- Your own messages no longer carry a blue fill and outline.
- A reply shows its quote on one quiet line.
- Messages that mention you get a soft accent highlight with a bar on the leading edge, as on desktop.

## Desktop

No desktop changes; desktop builds are versioned 2.7.4 with the rest of the release.

## Distribution

The coordinated tag workflow validates the release and publishes signed and notarized macOS downloads and updater metadata, Linux APT and pacman packages, Windows installers (checked to launch to the sign-in screen first), and the internal iOS TestFlight build.
