# Synara 2.1.28

Synara 2.1.28 fixes iOS rooms remaining unread after returning to the app.

The timeline now reconsiders its visible live tail when the scene becomes active.
It uses the newly delivered activity state throughout acknowledgement, rather
than the previous environment value captured by a SwiftUI callback. Existing
visibility, privacy, and exact-event checks still apply. Messages received in
the background or in an unopened room remain unread until viewed.

Live validation uses the signed iOS simulator and two dedicated test accounts
on a live homeserver. The probe checks the homeserver's fully-read marker and
private receipt, notification count, and the app's room-list indication. It
does not issue read receipts on the app's behalf.

The proof covers the reproduced foreground-transition failure. It does not
establish every read-state scenario or replace verification on physical devices.
